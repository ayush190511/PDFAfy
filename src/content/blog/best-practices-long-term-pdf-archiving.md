Digital preservation requires more than just storing files on disk. As storage hardware degrades and software formats evolve, organizations need a proactive archiving strategy. Here are the top best practices for long-term PDF document preservation.

---

## 1. Convert at Creation (OAIS Model)
Follow the Open Archival Information System (OAIS) framework. Convert documents into **PDF/A** at the time of ingestion or generation rather than waiting until files are 10 years old.

## 2. Standardize on PDF/A-2b for Business Records
Unless you have legacy mandates specifying PDF/A-1b or e-invoicing rules requiring PDF/A-3b, standardize your organization on **PDF/A-2b**. It supports transparency and layers while offering universal viewer compatibility.

## 3. Verify Compliance with Automated Audit Tools
Always validate converted files using ISO 19005 verification tools (such as VeraPDF or PDFAfy's built-in validator) to ensure XMP metadata, font streams, and color OutputIntents pass strict compliance checks.

## 4. Maintain Redundant Offsite Storage
Store PDF/A archives across multiple geographically separated cloud storage locations using the 3-2-1 backup strategy (3 copies, 2 different media types, 1 offsite copy).
